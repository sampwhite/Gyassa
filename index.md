Gyassa Software is Samuel White and Eva Cordes. We founded it in 2010, after years as the founding developers of the
Stellent content management system, which Oracle acquired and sold as Oracle Universal Content Management (UCM).

## What we do

**We are available for consulting.** Our focus is applications for forms and the work around them: intake, review,
approval, export, and reporting.

**We are building an open-source forms application.** KotlinDynamicRuntime distills what we have learned from many
years of developing this kind of software. It defines forms and workflows as configuration rather than code, it is
open source under the MIT license, and it was written by AI under our direction. Its product name is Gedra.

## Why "Gyassa"?

A gyassa is a lateen-sailed cargo boat of the Nile. Nautical names suggest going places and doing useful things,
and we want to make software that is useful and depended on for daily work.
[The longer story](personal/why-gyassa.html) is on the blog.
